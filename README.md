E-Commerce Sales Analysis Using SQL

📌 Project Overview

The E-Commerce Sales Analysis project focuses on analyzing sales transaction data to identify business trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and profitability. SQL is used to extract, clean, organize, and analyze data to generate meaningful insights that support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
Identify top-performing products and product categories.
Understand customer purchasing behavior and spending patterns.
Compare sales across different sales channels and payment methods.
Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
Evaluate order statuses, including delivered, cancelled, and returned orders.
Identify high-value customers and profitable product categories.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

SQL – Data querying and analysis
SQL Functions – Aggregate functions, CASE WHEN, date functions, and string functions
Advanced SQL – Subqueries, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), joins, and window functions
GitHub – Project documentation and version control

📊 Key Analysis Performed

Sales Analysis: Calculated total sales by month, year, category, and sales channel.
Product Analysis: Identified top-selling products and compared product sales.
Customer Analysis: Analyzed customer order frequency and total spending to identify valuable customers.
Profitability Analysis: Compared profit across product categories and classified products by profit level.
Payment Analysis: Examined order volumes by payment method.
Order Status Analysis: Evaluated delivered, cancelled, and returned orders.
Customer Satisfaction: Analyzed average customer ratings across product categories.
Business Insights: Identified sales trends and patterns to support business decision-making.

💡 Business Value

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform raw e-commerce transaction data into meaningful business insights. The analysis helps understand sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and areas that may require business improvement.
